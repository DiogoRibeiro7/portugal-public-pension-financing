# Internal replication review

This review reruns the manuscript-facing evidence gate from registered sources, processed
datasets, and registries before checking manuscript language.

## Scope

The review covers the current bounded article evidence only. It does not reconstruct missing
historical CGA component ledgers, payroll withholding totals, employer payroll bases, bank-level
transfer schedules, pension cash-flow paths, or full ESA restatement tables.

## Results

- The CGA 2011 account extract, ESA-95 transfer arithmetic, ESA-2010 classification, 2012 bank
  pension cost bridge, BPN 2012 legal amount, and 2011 bank-transfer accounting rows remain
  bounded but reproducible from the registered artifacts.
- The 2012 bank pension expenditure and current-transfer financing match at EUR 516.0 million in
  the current official-audit extract.
- The 2011 main bank-transfer panel and the 2012 BPN case remain separate perimeters.
- The 4 percent discount rate is treated as a statutory valuation assumption, not as an economic
  discount-rate conclusion.
- Present loaded manuscript terms now map to manuscript section-boundary IDs, and article-facing
  claims remain bounded by explicit blocked-inference rows.

## Alternative definitions

- ESA standard changes the reported deficit treatment: the current evidence supports an ESA-95
  headline-deficit effect and an ESA-2010 financial-transaction classification, not one universal
  deficit result.
- Combining the BPN 2012 case with the 2011 DL127 panel changes the transfer perimeter and must be
  explicitly labelled.
- Treating transferred assets as a financing resource without the pension-obligation cash-flow
  stream cannot classify the lifecycle operation as a public-finance gain or loss.
- Reserve counterfactuals change if additional contributions are financed as new expenditure
  rather than by reducing other transfers.

## Remaining blockers

The review leaves the following conclusions unresolved: employee remittance losses, employer
underpayment amounts, post-2006 public-worker reallocation magnitudes, combined CGA-RGSS balance
signs, bank-level net benefit or subsidy, and lifecycle public-finance gain or loss. Later
manuscript revisions require a rerun of the section-boundary and loaded-language checks.
