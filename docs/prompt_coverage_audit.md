# Prompt coverage audit — v0.2.0

The initial v0.1.0 repository contained 21 task prompts plus a roadmap. The scientific architecture was sound but the prompt pack was not yet complete enough for a publication-grade, multi-agent research workflow.

The v0.2.0 audit added coverage for:

- preregistered estimands and decision rules;
- accounting ontology and concept definitions;
- historical source discovery and explicit data-gap mapping;
- PDF/table extraction QA and transcription provenance;
- currency, units, nominal/real basis and accounting-basis normalization;
- official-source conflicts, revisions and uncertainty;
- public-sector employer/perimeter mapping;
- historical State-guarantee and Budget-transfer rules;
- RGSS contingency-rate decomposition and pension-only benchmarks;
- post-2006 public-worker cohort liabilities, not only contribution inflows;
- direct replication of the 2026 working-group claims;
- FEFSS return reconstruction and capitalization timing;
- CGA closed-scheme demographic decomposition;
- active bank-worker integration contribution flows;
- tracing of transferred bank-pension assets after 2011;
- actuarial identifiability and bounds when microdata are unavailable;
- bank-side liability relief from audited financial statements;
- BPN 2012 as a separate institutional case;
- a cross-entity stock-flow-consistent flow-of-funds matrix;
- public-debt and financing-cost effects of the bank transaction;
- academic literature and novelty mapping;
- data redistribution/licensing rules;
- manuscript language/claim auditing;
- journal-submission and independent-replication packaging.

All pre-existing task prompts were also expanded with a common execution contract, provenance rules, validation requirements and completion criteria.

The prompt pack now contains 48 numbered task prompts (`00` through `47`) plus `prompts/README.md`.
