# Reviewer methods appendix

This appendix gives reviewers a compact map of definitions, perimeters, robustness variants, and
current limitations.

## Definitions

- Employee contributions legally due are separate from payroll deductions and from contributions
  recorded as received.
- Employer contributions legally due are separate from employer contributions recorded as received
  and from economic benchmark gaps.
- State Budget transfers are separate from pension assets transferred from private funds.
- Institutional cash balances are separate from ESA national-accounts balances.
- Actuarial liabilities are separate from cash expenditure and public-debt financing effects.

## Accounting perimeters

- CGA budgetary accounts are tracked separately from RGSS and Social Security accounts.
- The 2011 DL127 banking-sector transfer is tracked separately from the 2012 BPN DL88 case unless
  a broader perimeter is explicitly declared.
- ESA-95 and ESA-2010 treatments are reported as different accounting classifications of the same
  transaction, not as one universal deficit result.
- Asset receipts, investment income, State financing, pension expenditure, and liability relief
  remain separate ledger dimensions.

## Robustness variants

- Bank liability valuation must vary discount-rate assumptions, including the statutory 4 percent
  reference, once pension cash-flow schedules are available.
- Counterfactual reserve accumulation must state the financing source and cannot both reduce State
  transfers and accumulate the same cash as untouched reserves unless that dual financing is
  explicitly modelled.
- Combined CGA-RGSS balance claims must report sensitivity to institutional perimeter and
  treatment of FEFSS flows.
- Bank-transfer conclusions must distinguish headline-deficit effects, gross-debt financing
  effects, and lifecycle pension-obligation costs.

## Current limitations

The current evidence does not support final conclusions on employee remittance losses, employer
underpayment amounts, post-2006 public-worker contribution reallocation, combined-balance signs,
bank-level net benefit, bank-transfer subsidy, or lifecycle public-finance gain or loss.

The current package remains partial until all release-readiness blockers in
`data/processed/release_reproducibility_audit.csv` are resolved and a clean sequential notebook
execution log is archived.
