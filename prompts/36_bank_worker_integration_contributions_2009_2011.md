# Reconstruct banking-worker integration contribution flows

## Objective
Separate the integration of active banking workers into RGSS from the later transfer of pensions already in payment.

## Tasks
- Reconstruct the legal sequence beginning with new bank workers entering RGSS and the CAFEB integration of active workers.
- Quantify contribution revenue entering Social Security from these populations where official data permit.
- Identify which contingencies remained under special arrangements.
- Separate contribution inflows from the 2011 pension-liability transaction.
- Reconcile against Social Security accounts and official programme-evaluation benchmarks.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/bank_worker_rgss_contributions.csv` and a legal-population mapping table.

## Acceptance criteria
No analysis of the 2011 pension transfer may count active-worker contribution inflows as transferred pension-fund assets.
