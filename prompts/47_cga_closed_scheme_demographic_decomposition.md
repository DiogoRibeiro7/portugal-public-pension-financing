# Decompose the mechanics of a closed CGA scheme after 2006

## Objective
Quantify how much of CGA's post-2006 cash deterioration follows mechanically from closure to new entrants rather than from earlier financing choices.

## Tasks
- Build contributor, pensioner and survivor counts by year where available.
- Track contributory payroll, average contribution, average pension and dependency ratio.
- Decompose annual balance change into contributor-count, wage/base, rate, pensioner-count, benefit and transfer effects using transparent identities.
- Mark policy-rate changes separately from demographic changes.
- Avoid causal claims when only accounting decomposition is identified.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/cga_closed_scheme_decomposition.csv` and companion figures.

## Acceptance criteria
The analysis must show the expected mechanical consequences of closure before attributing residual deterioration to historical underfunding.
