# Reproduce the 2026 Social Security working-group report claim by claim

## Objective
Independently reproduce the official/working-group calculations that triggered the current controversy.

## Tasks
- Locate and register the complete report, annexes, methodological notes and any released spreadsheets; use media only to identify claims when the primary document is unavailable.
- Extract every material quantitative claim into a public-claim registry.
- Reconstruct the exact accounting perimeter, years, contribution definitions and adjustment rules used by the report.
- Reproduce the calculations from primary underlying data rather than copying the report's final values.
- Mark each claim as reproduced, approximately reproduced, not reproduced or not identifiable.
- Record any methodological choices that are not fully specified in the report.

## Required claims to target
Include at least the cumulative post-2006 public-worker contribution estimate, the FEFSS-style capitalization claim, the share-of-FEFSS comparison, the combined CGA/previdential balance and the 2025 adjusted-deficit claim.

## Outputs
Create `evidence/public_claim_registry.csv`, `docs/working_group_2026_replication.md` and companion processed CSVs.

## Acceptance criteria
Do not tune assumptions until the published headline number appears. Report non-reproduction as a result.
