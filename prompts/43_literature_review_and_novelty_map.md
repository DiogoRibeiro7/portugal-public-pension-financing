# Build the literature review and novelty map

## Objective
Establish the paper's academic contribution rather than treating official reports and political debate as the literature review.

## Coverage
Search for work on Portuguese pension financing, CGA history, closed public pension schemes, pay-as-you-go versus funded accounting, government assumption of private pension liabilities, actuarial discounting, fiscal one-offs, ESA pension accounting and bank pension transfers.

## Tasks
- Record database, search date, query, inclusion decision and topic.
- Separate legal/institutional sources from academic literature.
- Build a literature map with research question, method, period, data, conclusion and relation to this paper.
- Identify nearest-neighbour papers and explicitly state what this project adds.
- Treat absence from a bounded search as evidence of absence, not proof of novelty.

## Outputs
Create `evidence/literature_map.csv`, `docs/literature_search_protocol.md` and a draft related-work synthesis.

## Acceptance criteria
No novelty claim may rely solely on the statement that the authors have not seen another paper doing the same thing.
