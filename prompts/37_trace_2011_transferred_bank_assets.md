# Trace the destination and treatment of 2011 transferred bank-pension assets

## Objective
Determine what happened to the assets delivered by bank pension funds after legal transfer to the State.

## Tasks
- Reconstruct final asset value and composition by institution where public records allow.
- Trace cash, Portuguese public-debt securities and other assets through Treasury/State accounting.
- Determine whether assets were liquidated, retained, consolidated against government debt or otherwise reclassified.
- Distinguish assets transferred to the State from resources held inside Social Security or FEFSS.
- Do not attribute investment income to Social Security unless ownership/management records support it.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/bank_asset_trace.csv` and `docs/bank_asset_trace_methodology.md`.

## Acceptance criteria
Long-run burden calculations must reflect the actual ownership and accounting treatment of transferred assets, not assume they formed a ring-fenced Social Security fund.
