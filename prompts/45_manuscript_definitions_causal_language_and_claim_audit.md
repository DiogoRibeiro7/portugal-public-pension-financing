# Audit manuscript definitions, causal language and legally loaded terms

## Objective
Prevent the final paper from turning accounting results into stronger legal or political claims than the evidence supports.

## Tasks
- Scan every use of terms such as `debt`, `underfunded`, `diverted`, `subsidy`, `loss`, `harmful`, `surplus`, `deficit`, `sustainable` and `artificial`.
- Require each use to map to a defined concept/estimand.
- Distinguish descriptive accounting decomposition from causal inference.
- Distinguish legal non-compliance from an economic counterfactual shortfall.
- Distinguish Social Security institutional burden from consolidated State burden.
- Flag sentences that imply motive or misuse without documentary evidence.

## Outputs
Create `paper/claim_language_audit.md` and update the claim registry.

## Acceptance criteria
No legally or politically loaded headline conclusion may survive solely because it is rhetorically plausible; it must be tied to a validated quantitative or legal claim.
