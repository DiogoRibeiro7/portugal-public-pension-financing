# Measure bank-side liability relief from audited financial statements

## Objective
Quantify the benefit received by participating banks using their own audited accounts rather than inferring benefit from the statute alone.

## Tasks
- Collect 2011-2012 annual reports/financial statements for participating institutions where public.
- Extract pension liabilities derecognized, assets surrendered, accounting gains/losses, capital effects and disclosed retained obligations.
- Reconcile institution-level disclosures with the aggregate State transaction.
- Distinguish accounting P&L gain, capital relief, liquidity effect and actuarial risk transfer.
- Flag institutions affected by merger, resolution or unavailable statements.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/bank_financial_statement_effects.csv` and source-level extraction evidence.

## Acceptance criteria
A claim that banks benefited materially must identify the measurable channel and net value, not simply the gross liability extinguished.
