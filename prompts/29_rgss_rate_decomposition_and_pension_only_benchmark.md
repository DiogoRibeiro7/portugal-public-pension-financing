# Decompose RGSS contribution rates into comparable pension-risk components

## Objective
Construct a defensible economic benchmark for comparing CGA employer financing with RGSS without treating the full RGSS employer rate as pension-only.

## Tasks
- Reconstruct legal RGSS worker/employer rates by period and contingency.
- Identify the portions associated with old age, invalidity and survivors versus unemployment, sickness, parental benefits and other risks.
- Map which risks were borne directly by public employers outside CGA.
- Build one or more comparable pension-risk contribution benchmarks.
- Keep the full RGSS rate only as a clearly labeled broad social-protection scenario.

## Outputs
Create `evidence/rgss_rate_decomposition.csv` and benchmark functions.

## Acceptance criteria
No manuscript comparison may say that a historical CGA employer paid `x percentage points less than required` unless `required` refers to the actual law; economic benchmarks must be labeled counterfactual.
