# Quantify public-debt and financing-cost effects of the bank pension transfer

## Objective
Evaluate the consolidated public-finance effect beyond the one-off ESA deficit treatment.

## Tasks
- Determine how transferred cash and government-bond assets affected State financing needs and public-debt measures.
- Build a counterfactual in which the State did not receive the transferred assets but also did not assume the transferred pension obligations.
- Estimate interest-cost implications using observed government borrowing costs, with sensitivity analysis.
- Avoid double counting government bonds that may consolidate differently after transfer.
- Keep debt, deficit, liquidity and actuarial liability as distinct quantities.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/bank_transfer_debt_financing_effects.csv`.

## Acceptance criteria
Any claim that the operation was beneficial or harmful to consolidated government must include both the asset/financing benefit and the pension-obligation cost when data permit.
