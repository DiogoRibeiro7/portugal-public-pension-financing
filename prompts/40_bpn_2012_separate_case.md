# Analyse the BPN-group 2012 pension transfer as a separate case

## Objective
Prevent Decree-Law 88/2012 from being silently pooled with the 2011 private-bank transaction when its institutional destination or financing differs.

## Tasks
- Reconstruct the exact population, assets, liabilities and receiving institution under the BPN-related transfer.
- Identify whether responsibilities moved to CGA, Social Security or another public entity.
- Reconcile State financing and accounting treatment separately.
- Determine whether the case belongs in the main quantitative panel or only as a sensitivity/appendix case.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/bpn_2012_pension_transfer.csv` and a separate claim registry section.

## Acceptance criteria
The main 2011 bank-transfer estimates must not include BPN values unless the manuscript explicitly defines a broader perimeter.
