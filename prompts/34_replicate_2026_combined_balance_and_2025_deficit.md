# Replicate the 2026 combined-balance and 2025-deficit claims

## Objective
Reconstruct the claim that CGA plus the Social Security previdential system is negative under the working group's chosen adjustments.

## Tasks
- Recover the report's exact formula and institutional perimeter.
- Identify all additions, exclusions, reclassifications and one-off adjustments.
- Reproduce the claimed annual series and specifically the 2025 value.
- Then calculate preregistered alternative perimeters without altering source data.
- Show which components change the sign of the balance and which only change magnitude.
- Keep bank-special-regime pension obligations visible as a separate sensitivity when relevant.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/combined_balance_replication_2026.csv` and a component bridge table.

## Acceptance criteria
The manuscript must distinguish `we reproduce the published definition` from `we endorse this as the economically correct definition`.
