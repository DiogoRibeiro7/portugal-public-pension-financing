# Model post-2006 public-worker cohorts and accrued RGSS pension rights

## Objective
Avoid treating post-2006 public-worker contributions to RGSS as pure revenue without associated future pension rights.

## Tasks
- Reconstruct new public-worker entrants to RGSS by year using official employment data where available.
- Estimate contribution bases and contribution inflows with uncertainty bounds where direct administrative totals are unavailable.
- Separate worker and employer contributions and pension-related versus non-pension components.
- Build a transparent cohort framework for accrued future pension rights; use aggregate bounds if microdata are unavailable.
- Report current-flow effect and intertemporal liability effect separately.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/public_worker_rgss_cohorts.csv` and `evidence/public_worker_liability_assumptions.csv`.

## Acceptance criteria
The analysis must never present the cumulative contributions of post-2006 public workers as a free improvement in sustainability without noting the pension rights simultaneously accumulated.
