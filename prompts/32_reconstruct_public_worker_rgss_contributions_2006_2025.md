# Reconstruct public-worker RGSS contributions from 2006 to 2025

## Objective
Independently estimate the contribution inflow generated by public employees entering RGSS after CGA closed to new subscribers.

## Tasks
- Prefer administrative contribution totals that identify public employers or public-sector workers.
- If unavailable, reconstruct from cohort counts, contributory remuneration and legal rates.
- Separate employee and employer contributions.
- Separate pension-related contributions from total social-protection contributions when comparing with CGA.
- Quantify uncertainty from missing 2006-2010 public-employment detail and alternative wage-base assumptions.
- Reconcile totals against aggregate RGSS contribution revenue so reconstructed public-sector contributions cannot exceed plausible totals.

## Outputs
Create `data/processed/public_worker_rgss_contributions_2006_2025.csv` with lower/base/upper estimates where needed.

## Acceptance criteria
Every estimated year must identify whether it is direct observation or reconstruction.
