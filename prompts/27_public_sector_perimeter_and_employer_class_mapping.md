# Map public-sector perimeter and employer classes over time

## Objective
Reconstruct which employers belonged to which legal contribution regime and statistical perimeter in each period.

## Tasks
- Build a timeline for central administration, regional/local administration, public institutes, public enterprises and other relevant employer classes.
- Map legal contribution rules to employer classes by effective date.
- Record restructurings, mergers, privatizations or reclassifications that change the observed contribution base.
- Distinguish employer legal status from national-accounts sector classification.

## Outputs
Create `evidence/employer_perimeter_registry.csv` and mapping utilities.

## Acceptance criteria
Employer-liability reconstruction must not apply one rate to entities that were subject to different legal regimes in the same year.
