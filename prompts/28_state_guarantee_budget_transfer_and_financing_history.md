# Reconstruct the State guarantee and Budget-transfer financing history

## Objective
Reconstruct how the State legally and practically financed CGA when explicit employer contributions were absent, partial or changing.

## Tasks
- Identify legal provisions governing State guarantees, balancing transfers, subsidies or budget appropriations to CGA.
- Build an annual series of transfer categories and their legal/accounting purpose.
- Distinguish employer contribution from balancing transfer and extraordinary transfer.
- Record whether transfer formulas were residual, fixed, appropriated ex ante or settled ex post.
- Identify changes in the relationship between State as employer and State as guarantor.

## Outputs
Create `evidence/state_financing_rule_registry.csv` and a documented historical timeline.

## Acceptance criteria
The paper must be able to explain why a transfer occurred before interpreting it as evidence of underfunding or deficit.
